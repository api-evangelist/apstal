# Apstal

> AI-first website analytics and visitor tracking platform. Replaces traditional dashboards with a natural language chatbot interface.

## Core Features

- **Analytics Chatbot**: Ask questions about traffic, drop-offs, conversions, and get instant answers in natural language.
- **Session Replays & UX Insights**: Visual session recording, heatmaps, rage clicks, and user path analysis.
- **Privacy-First & Lightweight**: Minimal latency tracking script, GDPR compliant.
